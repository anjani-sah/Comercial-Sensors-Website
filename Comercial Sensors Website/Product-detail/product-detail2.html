Definition:Our pH Sensor is an advanced analytical device designed to measure the hydrogen-ion activity in soil, determining its acidity or alkalinity. Featuring a pH-sensitive electrode, this sensor produces a voltage proportional to the soil's pH level, providing precise readings.
